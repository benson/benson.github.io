Pick an anime for us to watch

I cut the extra-sleepy stuff and kept shows that are easy to get into, light on fanservice, and good for a first anime night. Most are anime; one is a western animated AI wildcard that spiritually belongs here.

Fastest safe picks

1

Haikyu!!

Fun, sincere, barely any baggage. The safest first pick if you want momentum.

2

Delicious in Dungeon

Fantasy adventure plus cooking. Weird in a friendly way.

3

Spy x Family

Easy comedy, cute family setup, plenty of plot without being heavy.

Haikyu!!

A tiny, wildly determined volleyball kid joins a team and turns every point into an emotional event.

Pick this if: she likes underdog stories, team dynamics, comedy, or sports movies.

Heads up: lots of yelling and tournament intensity, but it is wholesome yelling.

Delicious in Dungeon

A dungeon party has to rescue a friend, but the practical solution is to cook and eat the monsters.

Pick this if: she likes fantasy, cooking shows, tabletop-game nonsense, or ensemble comedy.

Heads up: some monster grossness and danger, but the tone is mostly playful.

Spy x Family

A spy, an assassin, and a telepathic kid form a fake family and accidentally become adorable.

Pick this if: she wants something charming, funny, and easy to understand instantly.

Heads up: it has broad anime comedy, but it is very first-timer friendly.

Frieren: Beyond Journey's End

After the big fantasy quest is already over, an elf mage slowly learns what her old companions meant to her.

Pick this if: she wants gorgeous, thoughtful fantasy with bursts of excellent action.

Heads up: it is a gentle slow burn, so only pick it if contemplative sounds good.

Keep Your Hands Off Eizouken!

Three girls start making animation, and their imagination keeps turning the real world into a sketchbook.

Pick this if: she likes creative people, art-process shows, or fast visual invention.

Heads up: it is more eccentric than the others, in a fun way.

Pantheon

A bullied teen starts getting messages from her dead father, whose mind may have been uploaded to the cloud.

Pick this if: she likes Black Mirror-ish sci-fi, consciousness puzzles, tech conspiracies, and big existential questions.

Heads up: not anime, but close enough for this list. Adult, tense, and emotionally heavy.

Death Note

A brilliant student finds a notebook that can kill people, then starts a cat-and-mouse game with a detective.

Pick this if: she likes murder mysteries, mind games, and morally messy main characters.

Heads up: dark, intense, and melodramatic. Great gateway, but not chill.

Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood

Two brothers try to fix a terrible mistake in a world of alchemy, military conspiracies, and big feelings.

Pick this if: she wants the classic big adventure with jokes, tragedy, and a real ending.

Heads up: heavier themes, body horror, war, grief, and a longer commitment.

My vote

If we want the lowest-risk first night: Haikyu!!. If we want food and fantasy weirdness: Delicious in Dungeon. If we want darker and more addictive: Death Note or FMA:B.
