    how trophy seat works

    one real seat. eight blind choices.

    An anonymous 17Lands player drafted these exact packs and finished with seven wins and no more than two losses.

    You replay their first eight picks without seeing what they chose. Those packs are safe to reproduce: before pick nine, nothing you take can circle the table and affect a pack you receive.

    After that, the draft becomes a replay. You can inspect every later choice and the final recorded deck, but the app does not pretend those later packs would have stayed the same.

    Each seat comes from an individual public draft log on 17Lands. The app uses a fixed cached snapshot and makes no live data requests.
