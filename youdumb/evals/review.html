Admins can still reveal results elsewhere; disclose any prior exposure. Your ratings and notes are saved privately under a random browser reviewer ID, without a name. how to grade a case

You are checking what the answers demonstrate—not guessing how smart the writer is, or predicting the model’s score. A case is one set of five answers.

  1. Read the response beside each checklist. For every criterion, choose yes if the text demonstrates it and no if it does not. On a narrow screen, the response sits directly above its criteria.
  2. Work through one section at a time, or choose “show all sections.” Nothing is preselected. If you cannot reliably judge a section, mark its uncertainty box.
  3. Notes are optional—even for uncertain answers. Save only when ready: saving locks this case’s judgment and reveals its model comparison.

These numbers are rubric points, not school grades, percentiles, or IQ. A 50 does not mean “below average”; a 100 means the listed criteria were demonstrated, not that an answer is perfect in every way.

partial credit, corrections, and when to choose “uncertain”
  • Count each criterion once, only when the text supports it. One short sentence may earn several criteria. Do not invent a missing explanation or demand particular words.
  • Partial credit is calculated automatically from the criteria shown—not a general impression. You only decide whether each criterion is demonstrated.
  • Credit an explicit final correction. An earlier mistake does not cancel corrected reasoning, but do not silently repair a contradiction for the writer.
  • Choose no for a clearly absent criterion, even if other parts of the answer are good. Leaving yes/no unanswered is not the same as no; complete each row before saving.
  • Mark a dimension uncertain when missing, unreadable, or genuinely ambiguous text prevents a reliable judgment. It is not a middle score and does not count as zero. You can optionally note what made it hard to judge.
  • Ignore spelling, dialect, confidence, and fancy vocabulary. A wrong answer can still be clearly communicated; score each dimension independently.
